# Character model credits

This local game uses imported, rigged character models. These credits must travel with the assets.

## People — Ready Player Me

Human1.glb, Human2.glb and Human3.glb are avatars from the official [Ready Player Me VR Demo](https://github.com/readyplayerme/VR-Demo), under Assets/Ready Player Me/Avatars. They are renamed locally. The demo repository code has an MIT licence (RPM-DEMO-LICENSE.md). This does not replace the avatar-content licence.

brunette.glb is a Ready Player Me avatar distributed by [TalkingHead](https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead), whose README identifies the avatar as [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Ready Player Me avatar content is used here for this personal, noncommercial game. Obtain appropriate rights before commercial distribution.

Local modifications: per-character material colours, head-width variation, scale, visibility of accessories, skeletal poses, and procedural walking animation. Hair choices currently select a long-haired or bob-haired imported female model; the former individually constructed hair shapes are hidden.

## Wolves, foxes and horses — Quaternius

Wolf.gltf, Fox.gltf, Horse.gltf and Horse_White.gltf come from [Ultimate Animated Animals](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), by Quaternius, public domain under CC0. Original licence: QUATERNIUS-LICENSE.txt. Files obtained from the [public pack mirror](https://github.com/Tekh-ops/Ultimate-Animated-Animals-July-2021/tree/main/glTF).

Local modifications: runtime scale, orientation and idle/walk animation selection.

## Three.js

The GLTFLoader, SkeletonUtils and BufferGeometryUtils modules are from [Three.js r170](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170/examples/jsm). Imports are adjusted for local use. MIT licence: ../../vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

Cats, bears and birds currently retain the game's original procedural models. No replacement asset is claimed for those species.
